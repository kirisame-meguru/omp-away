The wave animation in lens-overlay.glsl is adapted from js/main.js in:
https://github.com/neeldedkawala/ios-siri-animation

The four-layer Intelligence palette, spindle envelope, phase offsets, harmonic
motion, thickness weights, speed, and glow use the upstream defaults. Integration
changes adapt coordinates, scale wave height with the existing audio envelope,
and retain the application's live-content refraction and glass composition.
No upstream images, GIFs, wallpapers, or device artwork are bundled.
